- Keep `/ipc` routes as compatibility redirects to the canonical public `/ipm` routes, because previously shared public links must remain valid.
- The full-process PDF export ("Exportar processo (PDF)") uses the vector engine in `src/lib/pdf/` (jsPDF text, not screenshots): SSP crest letterhead from `public/pdf/brasao-ssp.png`, "DESPACHO - CORREGPM"-style titles, and a footer with IPM/SINDPM reference, the PM emblem (`public/pdf/emblema-pm.png`) and "Página X de Y". The Portaria and the final act (especie `ipc`, exported as "SOLUÇÃO" with PARTES = denounced officers) follow the approved models with identification key/barcode. Do not replace it with html2canvas captures; extend `src/lib/pdf/processo-pdf.ts` instead.
- The old screenshot-based exporter (`exportProcessoCompleto`, "versão anterior" button) is kept temporarily as a fallback. Individual act downloads and the standalone Portaria still use the shared browser renderer (fixed A4 width, line-safe captures) until migrated; keep the approved standalone Portaria renderer separate.
- Public complaint protocol (denuncias.numero_protocolo, PMESP-BR-…) is the citizen's key and never appears on public IPM pages; internal staff screens identify cases by numero_procedimento (generated only by the instaurar_procedimento DB function) and fall back to the protocol with an 'Aguardando instauração' badge — keeps the citizen key private and numbering atomic.
- Minutas para assinatura (`/minutas`, `src/lib/minutas.functions.ts`): any internal user can forward an act from "Lançar ato" or the Portaria dialog to signers (corregedor_geral, subcorregedor_geral, corregedor_supervisor — list in the DB function `minuta_pode_assinar`). All writes go through SECURITY DEFINER functions (`encaminhar_minuta`, `assinar_minuta`, `devolver_minuta`, `reenviar_minuta`, `cancelar_minuta`); the `minutas` table has a read-only RLS policy. Signing inserts the movimento with the signer as prolator and the sender in `movimentos.escrivao_*`, which the PDF prints as "Escrivão" under the signature. Teor assembly is shared with "Lançar ato" in `src/lib/teor-ato.ts`.
- Ofícios (`/oficios`, `src/lib/oficios.functions.ts`, PDF in `src/lib/pdf/oficio-pdf.ts`): numbered `CORREGPM 001/360/26` per year by the DB function `oficio_expedir_interno` (number reserved only on expedition, never reused; cancelled ofícios keep it). Only corregedor_geral/subcorregedor_geral/corregedor_supervisor expedite directly; other internal users forward for signature (same signer list as minutas). The PDF reproduces the official letterhead (Times, crest + address column, no site name); the drafter is printed as "Encarregado". Linked processes get an "Expedido o Ofício nº …" evento with a PDF button on the timeline.
- TAC — Termo de Ajustamento de Conduta (`/tac`, `src/lib/tac.functions.ts`, PDF `gerarTacPdf` in `processo-pdf.ts`, adapter `src/lib/tac-pdf-dados.ts`): numbered `001/2026` per year, reserved only on celebration. Only the cúpula celebrates/ends; others forward for signature. Requisitos, obrigações padrão and the two fixed clauses live in `tac_config` (editable by the cúpula in "Configurar requisitos"); each TAC stores the text it was signed with. A linked IPM/SINDPM is suspended (evento on the timeline + banner); cumprido archives it, descumprido/cancelado resumes it and adds the suspended days to `acrescimos_dias`. TACs also appear in Minutas (category "TAC") and in the ficha funcional.
- Petições do denunciante (PDF) (`src/lib/peticoes.functions.ts` + server-only `src/lib/peticoes.server.ts`, UI `PeticoesUploader`/`PeticoesDenuncia`/`PeticoesManutencao`): the public form uploads PDFs (max 5 × 10 MB) straight to the private bucket `denuncia-pdfs` under `entrada/` with one-time signed upload URLs; on submit the server (service role) checks the `%PDF-` header, moves them to `{denuncia_id}/` and records them in `denuncia_peticoes` (RLS: visible to whoever sees the process). Files expire after 30 days: expired ones (and abandoned `entrada/` uploads > 24 h) are purged by `faxinaPeticoes` when staff open the Corregedoria, or by the cúpula in Manutenção; the row stays with `removido_em`. The full-process PDF opens with a "TERMO DE DENÚNCIA" (relato, informações adicionais and links in full, petition index) and, unless unchecked, joins each available petition after a "TERMO DE JUNTADA" page via pdf-lib (`src/lib/pdf/juntada-pdf.ts`), keeping the "Página X de Y" numbering continuous.
